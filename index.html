Privacy
Photos are analyzed on this phone and are never uploaded. Only numbers (and small thumbnails, if you turn that on) are stored in this browser. The app downloads its face-tracking model once, then works offline.

Healthy use
Bone-structure ratios barely change, and skin numbers move with light, sleep and routine. Scan about once a week, same place and same light, and judge the trend, not single scans. These are rough estimates, not medical advice or an attractiveness rating.
